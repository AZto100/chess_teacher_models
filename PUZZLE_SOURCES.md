# Puzzle positions and attribution

The playing screen deliberately hides source information and solution examples. The JSON retains provenance for editing and verification.

Levels 2-6 use published game positions compiled by Bill Harvey at wtharvey.com. Level 1 uses a legally verified training continuation of each level-2 position; these are not claimed to be moves actually played in the original game. Only factual positions, player/event identifiers, and move coordinates are reproduced; no explanatory prose or diagrams are copied.

All shipped positions have been independently checked with a bounded legal-move AND/OR search: the attacking side can force mate within N moves, and cannot force it in N-1. Some defences allow a faster mate.

Fullmove numbers of zero in some source FENs were normalised to 1. No pieces or side-to-move fields were changed.
